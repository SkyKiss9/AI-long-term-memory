## Long-running project continuity

- Use `$maintain-project-continuity` on the first project-work message, material decisions, and natural continuation or closeout (including “本对话收尾” and “下个对话继续”). Standalone Q&A does not require project bookkeeping.
- Ordinary starts check repository state and read the manifest and current handoff only. Recover the goal, relevant effective decisions and current boundary; use targeted timeline/source lookup for gaps, conflicts or a proposed reversal. Do not default to full-history reading or memory exams.
- Maintain a chronological notebook, not summaries of summaries. Record each substantive conversation or milestone briefly with its origin, decisions and known reasons, actual result and direct source. Distinguish user decisions, suggestions, acceptance, verification and cancellation. Append later changes without rewriting earlier history.
- Keep one authoritative current handoff identified by project rules/manifest. Refresh it from the current conversation and artifacts, removing stale current state while preserving history. Include relevant effective decisions, verification limits, next authorized action and any uncommitted-work disposition. Resolve competing entries from evidence, not timestamps.
- At closeout, stop expanding business work, reconcile the whole conversation with saved records, update only missing information, and safely commit attributable authorized changes after permitted checks. Read the skill's Git closeout reference; preserve unrelated staged/unstaged work. Do not blanket-stage, reset, clean or push to force a clean workspace. A labelled checkpoint is not business completion.
- If records and commits are already complete, do not duplicate them. If saving or committing fails, identify the remainder, reason and disposition truthfully. Explicit no-write/stop instructions override bookkeeping. Ending a conversation does not itself cancel unfinished work.
- New projects get records inside their own project. Old projects keep their records and are repaired/reactivated from their own workspace; do not batch-migrate or auto-unify them from elsewhere. Global memory is not substitute project evidence; missing sources remain explicit gaps.
- The AI owns lookup and write-back; the user need not maintain records or repeat settled decisions. Keep this work in the background and follow global communication preferences. Continuity does not authorize business changes, tests, deployment or work in another project. Structural checks do not prove faithful memory, safe commit attribution or user acceptance.
